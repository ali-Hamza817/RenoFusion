\subsubsection{Mechanistic Ablations}
Residual error correlation entries are $\rho \in [0.3842, 0.4619]$ for M1--M2, M1--M3, and M2--M3 off-diagonal pairs ($p < 0.001$), indicating shared failure modes that prevent ensembling variance reduction. Mean Dempster--Shafer cohort conflict is $\bar{K} = 0.4381$ (95\% CI [0.1245, 0.7892]) with 54.8\% high-conflict patients ($K > 0.40$). For SMOTE probability stretching: M2 Genomic AUROC without SMOTE is 0.6512; uncalibrated DST AUROC with SMOTE is 0.7449; uncalibrated DST AUROC without SMOTE is 0.7011, isolating a $+0.0438$ artifactual AUROC boost.

\subsubsection{RAG Score Distribution and Architectural Shrinkage}

Figure~\ref{fig:rag_scores} illustrates the raw retrieval score distributions across the three heterogeneous retrieval streams (BM25, Dense Cosine, Radiomic Similarity), demonstrating the fundamentally different score spaces that must be reconciled before fusion. BM25 scores are unbounded and right-skewed, dense cosine scores occupy $[-1, 1]$, and radiomic similarity scores cluster in $[0, 1]$. Ad-hoc Min-Max normalization and RRF compress these into a shared $[0, 1]$ interval without probabilistic calibration, which is the mechanistic origin of the Retrieval--Fusion Calibration Hazard.
